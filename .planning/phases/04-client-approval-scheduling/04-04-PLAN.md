<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 2: Live checkpoint — full Client approval/adjustment/scheduling round-trip</name>
  <action>Human walks through the 12-step live round-trip below across two role sessions (Client, then PM), confirming each of ROADMAP Phase 4's 6 success criteria against a real running app — no automated test can substitute for this since it requires genuine cross-role UI observation (APR-04's manual-only verification, 04-VALIDATION.md).</action>
  <what-built>
    The full Client Approval & Scheduling loop: a real `/client` board (queue + history tabs), one-click approve, comment-required adjustment (bouncing the card back to `producao`), the PM seeing that comment on the card, the PM registering a publish date once the card reaches `agendamento`, and the "Pronto para publicar" badge appearing identically on both the PM board and the Client's own history tab.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Requires two role sessions (a PM/Admin account and a Client account already provisioned for a test client with at least one card that has reached `revisão interna` and cleared its checklist — reuse an existing test client from this session's prior verifications where possible, per this project's established "clean up test data after" convention).

    1. As PM, advance an existing card through to `aprovação do cliente` (checklist gate cleared) via the normal board flow — call it Card A.
    2. As Client, log in and land on `/client`. Confirm the "Para revisar" tab shows Card A with badge "Aguardando sua revisão", and shows a count suffix in the tab trigger (e.g. "Para revisar (1)").
    3. Open Card A's detail Dialog as Client. Confirm ONLY title/description/attachments are visible — no checklist state, no Responsável/Designer-Mídia, no Canal badge.
    4. Click "Aprovar" on Card A. Confirm toast "Conteúdo aprovado.", the Dialog closes, Card A disappears from "Para revisar" and appears in "Histórico" with badge "Aprovado" (no publish date set yet).
    5. As PM, confirm Card A now sits in the `Agendamento` column on the board.
    6. As PM, advance a second card (Card B) to `aprovação do cliente` the same way as step 1.
    7. As Client, refresh `/client`. Confirm Card B appears in "Para revisar". Open its Dialog, type a comment in "Sua avaliação" (e.g. "Trocar a imagem de capa"), confirm "Solicitar ajuste" is disabled with an empty comment and enables once text is entered, then click it. Confirm toast "Ajuste solicitado.", Card B disappears from the Client's queue entirely (does not appear in Histórico either — it bounced backward, out of the Client's stage-filtered view).
    8. As PM, confirm Card B is now in the `Produção` column, and that opening its detail Dialog shows a "Comentário do cliente" block with the EXACT text the Client typed in step 7, directly on the card (not a separate document/screen).
    9. As PM, advance Card B again through `Revisão interna` — confirm its checklist items retain whatever completion state they already had from the FIRST pass (not reset/unchecked) — then to `Aprovação do cliente` again.
    10. As PM, open Card A's (from step 4) detail Dialog. Confirm a "Data de publicação" field is visible (Card A is in `Agendamento`). Set a date/time, save. Confirm "Card atualizado." toast, and a "Pronto para publicar" badge now appears both at the top of the Dialog and on Card A's own board-card badge stack.
    11. As Client, revisit `/client`'s "Histórico" tab. Confirm Card A now shows badge "Pronto para publicar" (not "Aprovado") with the formatted publish date/time shown in its meta line.
    12. Confirm a Client account cannot navigate to `/pm/board` or `/editor` (existing middleware redirect, sanity check only — no new code exercised here).

    Clean up any test cards/clients created solely for this checkpoint afterward, per this project's established convention.
  </how-to-verify>
